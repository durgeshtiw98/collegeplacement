## Definitions

Eligible placement percentage uses unique eligible placed students / eligible students. Analytical eligibility is CGPA ≥ 6 and zero backlogs, with unknown values excluded from that denominator. All-student placement percentage is reported separately. Accepted/joined offers establish observed placement. Salary summaries use known positive annual INR CTC for accepted/joined offers at offer grain.

Company selection rates use selected applications / applications, including withdrawals in the denominator. Drive participation uses known attended registrations / all registrations, with unknown attendance disclosed. N/A is preserved for empty denominators and absent compensation.
