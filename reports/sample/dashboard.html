How to read these numbers

Placed studentA unique student with at least one accepted or joined offer. Other offer states do not establish placement.
Analytical eligible cohortCGPA ≥ 6.0 with zero backlogs. This proxy is separate from company-specific eligibility.
Company and drive filtersThe student cohort denominator stays fixed; outcomes are restricted to the selected opportunities.
Salary and coverageMean, median and maximum of known accepted/joined offer CTC. Missing amounts remain unknown.
Missing values and privacyN/A means unavailable, including a zero denominator. Data is embedded locally; no network requests are made.
Scope and limitsSnapshot evidence supports descriptive associations, not causal claims or future placement predictions.
